## Strategic Additions Needed

### 1. Add "Why Most Teams Can't Fix This Themselves" Section

Insert before audit takeaways:

```
why most teams can't fix this themselves

Here's what usually happens when I point out these issues to B2B SaaS teams:

The product lead sees it immediately. "Oh god, you're right—we never actually say what we do."

They forward it to their PMM, who replies: "I know. I've been saying this for months. But I'm also launching the new feature, redoing the demo deck, supporting sales on the enterprise deal, and rewriting the case studies. Homepage is Q3."

Meanwhile, the sales team keeps asking why inbound leads are confused. And the problem compounds.

The issue isn't that your PMM doesn't know how to fix it. They probably do. The issue is they're underwater, and homepage copy keeps getting deprioritized because it's "just copy" and there's always something more urgent.

But here's what that delay costs you: if 10,000 people visit your homepage this month and your conversion rate is 0.5% (50 demos), but it should be 2% (200 demos)—you're losing 150 qualified conversations every month you wait.

That's not a Q3 problem. That's a this-week problem.
```

**Why This Works**:
- Validates their situation ("our PMM is overloaded" → "yes, that's normal")
- Creates urgency (you're losing 150 demos/month RIGHT NOW)
- Implies external help is the solution (without saying it directly)

### 2. Rewrite Your CTA to Position as "Fresh Eyes"

**Current CTA**:
> "Interested in how your website will hold up? I'm doing free 30-minute homepage teardowns for B2B SaaS companies."

**Problems**:
- "Interested in how your website will hold up" = low stakes, curiosity
- "I'm doing free teardowns" = sounds like you're practicing
- No urgency, no clear outcome

**Option A (Direct)**:
```
Think your homepage might be costing you conversions?

I'm offering free 30-minute teardowns for B2B SaaS teams. I'll walk through your homepage using this exact framework, show you what's breaking, and give you a rewrite you can hand directly to your design team.

No pitch, no strings—just a clear diagnosis from someone who's not drowning in your Q2 roadmap. [Book here]
```

**Option B (Softer, for Overwhelmed PMMs)**:
```
If your PMM keeps saying "I know, it's on my list"—I can help.

Book a free 30-minute teardown and I'll audit your homepage using this framework, give you specific rewrites, and create a one-pager your PMM can actually use (instead of adding "fix homepage" to their already-impossible backlog).

Think of it as hiring fresh eyes for 30 minutes. [Book here]
```

**Option C (For Product/Sales Leads)**:
```
Losing demos because prospects don't understand what you do?

I'll audit your homepage in 30 minutes (free) and show you exactly what's costing you conversions—with specific fixes you can hand to your team today, not Q3.

Limited slots available. [Book here]
```

### 3. Add "Who This Is For" Qualifier

Right before your CTA:

```
This teardown is for you if:
- You're getting traffic but demo requests are lower than they should be
- Prospects keep asking "wait, what do you actually do?" in discovery calls
- Your PMM knows the homepage needs work but it keeps getting bumped for other priorities
- You need an outside perspective (and a finished rewrite) you can implement this month, not Q3

Not a fit if:
- You just redesigned your homepage in the last 60 days (give it time to work)
- You're pre-launch and don't have conversion data yet
- Your conversion rate is already 3%+ (you're doing fine)
```

**Why This Works**:
- Qualifies the right people (traffic but low conversions, overwhelmed PMM)
- Creates FOMO (limited slots)
- Disqualifies the wrong people (shows you're not desperate for any lead)

### 4. Sprinkle "External Perspective" Language Throughout

**In your audit examples, add observations like**:

**After Hex.tech rewrite**:
> "When you're inside a company, you stop seeing what's obvious to outsiders. Hex's team probably debated this headline for weeks—but they forgot that 'AI analytics platform for your whole team' sounds exactly like Amplitude, Tableau, and Looker. Fresh eyes catch this in 5 minutes."

**After Statsig rewrite**:
> "I guarantee someone at Statsig has flagged this internally. But when you're juggling a product launch, competitive intel, and sales enablement, homepage copy falls to the bottom of the list. That's exactly when you need someone whose only job for 30 minutes is to fix your homepage."

**Why This Works**:
- Normalizes needing external help
- Positions you as "fresh eyes" vs. "expert they don't have"
- Implies their PMM isn't bad, just busy

### 5. Consider Adding a "What You'll Get" Box

Right above your CTA:

```
what you get in a 30-minute teardown

✅ Your homepage graded using the 3-question framework (What/Who/Why)
✅ Specific diagnosis of what's costing you conversions
✅ Rewritten hero section + subheadline you can use immediately
✅ A one-pager your design/marketing team can implement without another meeting

What you won't get: A sales pitch for a $15K rebrand project. This is a focused, tactical fix.

Time investment: 30 minutes on a call. That's it.
```

**Why This Works**:
- Removes objections (not a sales pitch, not a huge time sink)
- Shows concrete deliverable (one-pager they can hand off)
- Positions this as tactical, not strategic (fast fix, not months-long project)

## Revised Structure Recommendation

Here's the flow that would work best for your goal:

1. **Opening** → Reframe around THEIR pain (low conversions, not your design journey)
2. **The problem** → "Here's what most homepages get wrong" (3 questions)
3. **Proof** → Your graded examples (keep as-is, they're good)
4. **Why this matters** → "Why most teams can't fix this themselves" (new section)
5. **The fix** → Audit takeaways
6. **Who this is for** → Qualifier section
7. **CTA** → "Book a teardown" with clear deliverables
