07Strategic Insight

Miovision's real ICP is not "smart cities." That is an aspiration, not a buyer. The real ICP is the team responsible for operating traffic signals across a jurisdiction, whether that team sits inside a city, a DOT, a transit agency, or a consulting firm hired to do it. That single job is what unifies an otherwise sprawling product line, and it is the thing the homepage headline refuses to say plainly. Name the job in the buyer's own words, put the company on the category shelf the buyer actually shops, and the specificity your proof already earned finally shows up where it matters most: the first sentence.
