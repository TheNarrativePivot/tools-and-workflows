## Active Rules

*Seeded from Issue 3 (homepage audit post): the external strategy feedback received, and which of its suggestions Neera accepted vs. explicitly rejected. The rejections are as instructive as the acceptances.*

### Structure and framing

1. **Open pain-first, not story-first.** Issue 3's opening was rewritten from a personal-story lead to a pain lead (visitor numbers, lost conversions, team finger-pointing). Personal moments can appear, but the reader's problem opens the post. *(Evidence: issue-03, implemented change)*

2. **Acknowledge organizational reality, don't just diagnose tactics.** The highest-value addition to Issue 3 was the "why teams can't fix this themselves" section: the PMM is underwater, homepage keeps getting deprioritized, the problem compounds. Posts land harder when they name why the reader's team is stuck, not just what is wrong. *(Evidence: issue-03)*

3. **Make delay costs concrete.** "You're losing 150 qualified conversations every month you wait" beats "this costs you conversions." When claiming a cost, do the arithmetic in front of the reader. *(Evidence: issue-03 feedback, accepted)*

### What Substack posts never include (rejected patterns)

4. **No qualifier sections.** "This is for you if / not a fit if" blocks were proposed and explicitly rejected as too sales-heavy for Substack. *(Evidence: issue-03, rejected with reasoning)*

5. **No "what you'll get" boxes.** Same rejection, same reason. Substack demonstrates expertise; it does not package an offer. *(Evidence: issue-03, rejected)*

6. **No urgency or scarcity tactics.** "Limited slots available" was proposed and rejected. *(Evidence: issue-03, rejected)*

7. **CTA is one light line at the end, maximum.** The work sells; the CTA just opens a door. No stakes-raising, no outcome-promising CTA blocks. *(Evidence: issue-03, standing decision)*

8. **Don't over-explain why the reader needs external help.** The organizational-reality section implies it; saying it directly was rejected. Readers can connect that dot themselves. *(Evidence: issue-03, rejected "external perspective" language)*
